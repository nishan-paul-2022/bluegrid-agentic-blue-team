# 🛍️ @-track-04-online-commerce-ecommerce

*Driving SME growth through AI-driven marketplace optimization and intelligence.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Empowering SMEs:** Levelling the playing field for small businesses with enterprise-grade AI tools.

### 🔍 Core Focus Areas
- 📉 **Demand Forecasting:** Predicting what will sell before it's ordered.
- 🏷️ **Dynamic Pricing:** Real-time price optimization based on market elasticity.
- 🎯 **Recommendation Systems:** Hyper-personalized shopping experiences.
- 📊 **SME Dashboards:** Actionable insights for business owners.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations & Elements
> [!TIP]
> Focus on ROI impact—how does your AI directly increase revenue for an SME?

- **Full Stack + ML:** Integration of time-series forecasting and RL.
- **GraphRAG:** Using user-product affinity graphs for recommendations.
- **API Integration:** Plug-and-play connections for bKash, Shopify, and Daraz.
- **Scalability:** SaaS-ready designs for multi-merchant onboarding.
