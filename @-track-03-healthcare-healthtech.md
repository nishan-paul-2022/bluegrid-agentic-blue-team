# 🏥 @-track-03-healthcare-healthtech

*Advancing Public & Maternal health through Accessible, Ethical, and AI-enabled care.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Empathetic AI:** Bridging the gap in rural healthcare with ethical AI that assists both patients and field workers.

### 🔍 Core Focus Areas
- 🤰 **Maternal Companions:** 24/7 AI support for pregnancy and child care.
- 🚑 **AI Triage:** Instant risk assessment to prioritize critical cases.
- 🛡️ **Preventive Systems:** Early detection of health complications.
- 📡 **Rural Telehealth:** AI that works even in low-connectivity zones.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations & Elements
> [!CAUTION]
> Ethical safeguards and clinical validation are non-negotiable in this track.

- **Predictive Modeling:** Early warning systems for hypertension, anemia, etc.
- **Knowledge Graphs:** Integrating WHO and DGHS guidelines for accuracy.
- **Offline Resilience:** SMS/IVR fallbacks and local LLM deployment.
- **Data Privacy:** Ensuring sensitive health data is anonymized and secure.

## 💡 Example: MaaCare AI
> "Every mother deserves an AI guardian."

**What Makes it Win?**
- **Safety-First:** Uses WHO/DGHS knowledge graphs for explainable outputs.
- **Offline-First:** Local LLM + SMS fallback for rural mothers.
- **Early Warning:** Predictive engine for common pregnancy risks.
