# 🎓 @-04-track-01-education-edtech

*AI-Powered Personalized Learning Systems for a Global Market.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Adaptive & Inclusive:** Building AI ecosystems that provide high-quality, personalized education regardless of geography or bandwidth.

---

## 🔍 Track Details
### 📍 Core Focus Areas
- **Adaptive Tutors:** AI that evolves with the learner.
- **Teacher Copilots:** Automating admin and grading.
- **Career Engines:** Mapping skills to jobs.
- **Multilingual Support:** Breaking language barriers in education.

### ⚠️ Problem Scope
- Learning inequality & lack of personalization.
- Skill-employment mismatch.
- Overcrowded classrooms & limited teacher time.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations
- **User Profiling:** Deep analytics on student gaps.
- **Adaptive Models:** Dynamic difficulty adjustment.
- **RAG Content Retrieval:** Contextual intelligence from curriculum sources.

---

## 🏆 Challenges & Winning Formula
| Challenge | Winning Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Adaptive AI Tutor** | Adaptive learning loop + measurable outcomes. |
| **AI Exam Prep Engine** | Accuracy + personalization + feedback loops. |
| **Remedial Engine** | Focus on rural/low-performing users. |
| **Multimodal Assistant** | Accessibility + Bangla voice support. |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Decision intelligence for parents/teachers. |

---

## 💡 Example: ShikkhaAI
> [!TIP]
> **What Wins:** A Bangla-first, offline-capable tutor that uses RAG to pull from NCTB past papers and provides a teacher copilot for lesson plans.

---

- ✅ Learning improvement %
- ✅ Dropout reduction rates
