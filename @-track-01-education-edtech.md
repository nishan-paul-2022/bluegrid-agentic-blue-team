# 🎓 @-track-01-education-edtech

*Empowering the next generation through Adaptive, Inclusive, and Scalable AI learning ecosystems.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Transforming Education:** Moving from "one-size-fits-all" to high-precision, personalized learning that reaches every corner of Bangladesh.

### 🔍 Core Focus Areas
- 🧑‍🏫 **Adaptive Tutors:** AI that learns how you learn.
- 📝 **AI Assessments:** Beyond multiple-choice; understanding student reasoning.
- 👨‍🏫 **Teacher Copilots:** Automating admin so teachers can focus on mentoring.
- 🚀 **Career Engines:** Mapping learning to global employment opportunities.
- 📜 **Micro-credentials:** Verifiable, skill-based certification.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations & Elements
> [!IMPORTANT]
> All systems must demonstrate clear personalization logic and curriculum alignment.

- **User Profiling:** Deep intelligence on student pace and gaps.
- **RAG Integration:** Context-aware retrieval from NCTB and global datasets.
- **Bangla Support:** Native language integration for maximum accessibility.
- **Offline Resilience:** Utilizing local LLMs for low-bandwidth rural environments.

## 💡 Example: ShikkhaAI
> "The goal isn't just to teach; it's to ensure every student masters the concept."

**What Makes it Win?**
- **End-to-End Loop:** Student → Profiling → RAG → LLM Reasoning → Adaptive Tasks.
- **Offline Mode:** Local LLM (Ollama) + cached RAG for rural connectivity.
- **Teacher Copilot:** Automatic lesson plans and parent reports.
