# ⚡ @-14-infinity-5x-prompt-challenge

*Prompt to Production: Five Prompts. Infinite Possibilities.*

---

## 🎯 The Objective
Demonstrate the power of modern AI-native development. Build a functional AI application by issuing **no more than five structured prompts**.

> [!TIP]
> It's not about speed; it's about clarity of reasoning and mastery of context engineering.

---

## 🛠️ The Challenge Details
- **Allowed Tools:** Lovable or Cursor.
- **Constraints:** Maximum 5 prompts to generate architecture, logic, and UI.
- **Output:** A functioning prototype with a clear AI reasoning pipeline.

---

## 🧪 Skills Being Tested
1. **Context Engineering:** Providing the right information to the LLM.
2. **System Thinking:** Designing the workflow before typing.
3. **Orchestration:** Guiding AI agents toward a meaningful outcome.

---

## 📊 Evaluation Focus
| Category | What Judges Look For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Prompt Quality** | Precision, structure, and intent. |
| **System Coherence** | How well the generated parts work together. |
| **Reasoning Clarity** | Is the AI logic sound and explainable? |
| **Application Effectiveness** | Does the prototype solve the target problem? |

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This challenge reflects how leading AI startups build software—through precise intelligent orchestration rather than manual coding cycles.
