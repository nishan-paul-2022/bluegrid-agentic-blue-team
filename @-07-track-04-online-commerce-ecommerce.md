# 🛍️ @-07-track-04-online-commerce-ecommerce

*AI-Driven Marketplace Optimization for SMEs.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**SME Intelligence:** Empowering small businesses with AI tools for demand forecasting, pricing, and automation.

---

## 🔍 Track Details
### 📍 Core Focus Areas
- **Demand Forecasting:** Inventory optimization.
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Real-time pricing intelligence.
- **SME Dashboards:** Simplified business analytics.
- **Recommendation Engines:** Hyper-personalized commerce.

### ⚠️ Problem Scope
- Stock inefficiency and low margins.
- Poor analytics and limited forecasting capabilities.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations
- **Time-Series ML:** Models for demand prediction.
- **Optimization Engines:** For pricing and inventory.
- **API Integration:** Connecting with bKash, Shopify, Daraz.

---
