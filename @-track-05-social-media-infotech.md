# 🛡️ @-track-05-social-media-infotech

*Ensuring digital integrity through AI for Authenticity, Trust, and Information Integrity.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Truth in the Age of AI:** Building systems that validate, verify, and filter digital content to restore public trust.

### 🔍 Core Focus Areas
- 🕵️ **Fact-Checking:** AI engines that verify content against trusted sources.
- 🎭 **Deepfake Detection:** Vision models that identify manipulated media.
- 🔇 **Misinformation Filters:** Real-time identification of false narratives.
- ⚖️ **Trust Scoring:** Credibility ranking for content and sources.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations & Elements
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Transparency and "Explainable AI" are critical. Why did the AI flag this content?

- **NLP Pipelines:** Advanced text analysis for sentiment and bias.
- **Vision Models:** Detecting artifacts in AI-generated images and videos.
- **Graph-Based Trust:** Mapping source credibility across the web.
- **Public Dashboards:** Real-time alerts for emerging misinformation trends.
