## 📍 1. Acceptance of Terms
By registering, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to comply with this Code. If you do not agree, you must not participate.

---

## 🎯 2. Purpose
Ensures fair competition, ethical development, professional conduct, and data responsibility.

---

## 🤝 3. Professional Conduct
- **Respect:** Act respectfully toward organizers, mentors, and fellow teams.
- **Zero Tolerance:** No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination (Gender, Religious, Ethnic, or Racial).
- **Disqualification:** Violations result in immediate removal.

---

## 🤖 4. Original Work & AI Usage
- **Originality:** All work must be created by the registered team.
- **AI Tools:** Encouraged but must be **explainable and documented**.
- **Prohibited:** Plagiarism, fabricated demos, or fake results.

---

## ⚖️ 5. Responsible AI
- **Data:** Use legally sourced data and avoid harmful applications.
- **Privacy:** Anonymize all personal/health data.
- **No Liability:** Organizers are not liable for participant misuse of data.

---

## 🔐 6. Intellectual Property (IP)
- **Ownership:** Participants retain **full IP ownership**.
- **License:** You grant organizers a non-exclusive license to review and showcase your project for promotional purposes.

---

By selecting “I Agree,” you confirm this is a **legally binding digital agreement**.
