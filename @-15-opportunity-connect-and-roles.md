# 🤝 @-15-opportunity-connect-and-roles

*Converting Innovation into Real Career and Startup Opportunities.*

---

## 🔗 Opportunity Connect
A structured platform for pre-scheduled 1:1 meetings with ecosystem stakeholders.

### 📍 Meeting Types
- **Recruiter Interviews:** Job and internship offers from tech companies.
- **Investor Discussions:** Early-stage funding and incubation for startup concepts.
- **Higher Studies Guidance:** Research pathways and scholarship advice.
- **Global Mentorship:** Feedback from NRB experts and global practitioners.

---

## 👥 Stakeholder Roles
| Role | Primary Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Participants** | Build AI-native systems and deliver working prototypes. |
| **Mentors** | Guide architecture and strategy (Do NOT write code). |
| **Judges** | Evaluate based on the 100-point rubric. |
| **Organizers** | Provide platform, logistics, and global access. |

---

## 🏗️ Team Execution Roles (Product Model)
Every team should cover these 5 core responsibilities:
1. **Team Leader:** Vision and milestone coordination.
2. **Business Analyst / Data Scientist:** Problem definition and AI logic.
3. **UI/UX Developer:** User experience and accessibility (Bangla-first).
4. **Backend / Database Engineer:** APIs, LLM integration, and data pipelines.
5. **Communication Lead:** Demo storytelling and documentation.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Team Composition Tips:** Include women (diversity improves innovation) and students from diverse backgrounds (Polytechnic/Madrasah) for grounded innovation.
