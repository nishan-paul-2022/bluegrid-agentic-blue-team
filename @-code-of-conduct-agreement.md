# ⚖️ @-code-of-conduct-agreement

*Ensuring integrity, professionalism, and ethical AI development.*

---

## 🤝 Participation Agreement
By registering for **THE INFINITY AI BUILDFEST 2026**, all participants agree to uphold the highest standards of professional conduct and ethical development.

### 📜 Purpose of the Code
- ✅ **Fair Competition:** Ensuring a level playing field for all teams.
- ✅ **Ethical AI:** Developing systems that help, not harm.
- ✅ **Data Responsibility:** Treating all datasets with privacy and care.
- ✅ **Global Integrity:** Maintaining professional collaboration standards.

---

## 🚫 Professional Conduct Standards
Organizers maintain **ZERO TOLERANCE** for the following:
- ❌ **Harassment:** Verbal, physical, or digital intimidation.
- ❌ **Discrimination:** Based on gender, religion, ethnicity, or race.
- ❌ **Plagiarism:** Fabricated demos, fake results, or unauthorized copying.

> [!CAUTION]
> Violation of these standards will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the event.

---

## 🤖 Original Work & AI Usage
- **Permitted:** Use of LLMs, automation, and generative models is highly encouraged.
- **Requirement:** All AI usage must be **explainable and documented**.
- **Prohibited:** Teams cannot submit fabricated demos or simulated outputs.

---

## 🔒 Data Protection & Privacy
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Participants are solely responsible for ensuring lawful data usage.

1. **Anonymization:** All personal/health data must be anonymized.
2. **Compliance:** Solutions must follow applicable data protection laws.
3. **No Misuse:** Organizers are not liable for participant misuse of third-party data.

---

## 💡 Intellectual Property (IP)
- **Ownership:** Participants retain **full ownership** of all IP developed.
- **License:** You grant organizers a non-exclusive license to showcase your demo and project summaries for promotional purposes.

---

## 🌍 NRB Collaboration
- **Transparency:** All contributions from Non-Resident Bangladeshi (NRB) experts must be transparently documented.
- **Fairness:** NRBs may advise but cannot influence judging outcomes directly.
