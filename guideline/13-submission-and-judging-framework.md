# ⚖️ @-13-submission-and-judging-framework

*Measuring Innovation, Technical Depth, and Real-World Impact.*

---

## 📝 Submission Framework
Teams must submit a structured package to demonstrate engineering maturity.

### 📍 Key Components
- **Problem Definition:** Target users and the real-world need.
- **AI Architecture:** Input → Intelligence Core → Output.
- **Data Strategy:** Reliable and responsible data usage.
- **Scalability Roadmap:** Future growth and deployment pathway.
- **Ethical Safeguards:** Bias mitigation and privacy.

---

## ⏱️ Demo Presentation (4 Minutes)
Each team has exactly 4 minutes to communicate their vision:
1. **0:00 – 0:40:** Problem Definition.
2. **0:40 – 1:30:** Solution Overview.
3. **1:30 – 2:40:** Live System Demonstration.
4. **2:40 – 3:20:** AI Architecture Explanation.
5. **3:20 – 4:00:** Impact & Roadmap.

---

## 📊 Judging Criteria (100 Points)
| Criteria | Weight | What Judges Evaluate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Innovation** | 20% | Originality and transformative AI application. |
| **Technical Execution** | 20% | Architecture quality and system robustness. |
| **Business Model** | 20% | Monetization and global readiness. |
| **Real-World Impact** | 20% | Problem relevance and ethical safeguards. |
| **Scalability** | 10% | Deployment feasibility and NRB collaboration. |
| **Presentation** | 10% | Clarity, structure, and confidence. |

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Final Principle:** Projects are evaluated independently against the rubric, not comparatively. We reward AI-native engineering and ethical responsibility.
