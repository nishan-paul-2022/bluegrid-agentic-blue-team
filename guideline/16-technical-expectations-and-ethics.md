# 💻 @-16-technical-expectations-and-ethics

*Building Responsibly with a Global Engineering Mindset.*

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations
Teams must demonstrate **AI-native design thinking**:
- **Vibe Coding:** Agile, prompt-driven development.
- **Explainability:** Why does the AI make certain decisions?
- **Deployment Feasibility:** Is this ready for the real world?
- **Preferred Tech:** RAG pipelines, MCP orchestration, Multimodal models.

---

## ⚖️ Ethics & Responsible AI
Innovation must be balanced with accountability.

### 📍 Mandatory Guidelines
- **Lawful Data:** Use only legally sourced or scraped data.
- **Transparency:** No "black box" solutions; explain the logic.
- **Bias Mitigation:** Actively check for and reduce model bias.
- **Honesty:** No misleading demos or faked results.

---

## 💡 Intellectual Property (IP)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Ownership:** Participants retain **full IP ownership** of all solutions developed during the BuildFest.

---

## 🛑 Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **AI as a Gimmick:** Adding a chatbot where it's not needed.
2. **Ignoring Real Data:** Using hardcoded/fake data instead of scrapers/APIs.
3. **No Localization:** English-only UI limits adoption in Bangladesh.
4. **Overbuilding:** Focus on ONE core use case that works perfectly.
