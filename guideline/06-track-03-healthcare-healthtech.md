# 🏥 @-06-track-03-healthcare-healthtech

*AI-Augmented Public & Maternal Health Systems.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Theme
**Ethical & Accessible Care:** Leveraging AI to bridge gaps in rural healthcare with safe, clinically validated solutions.

---

## 🔍 Track Details
### 📍 Core Focus Areas
- **Maternal Companions:** AI for pregnancy and maternal support.
- **AI Triage:** Instant risk assessment and prioritization.
- **Preventive Systems:** Early detection and predictive care.
- **Rural Telehealth:** Offline-resilient healthcare delivery.

### ⚠️ Risk Areas
- Health misinformation.
- Rural access gaps.
- Data privacy and ethical safeguards.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Expectations
- **Predictive Modeling:** Early warning systems for complications.
- **Knowledge Graphs:** Integrating WHO/DGHS medical guidelines.
- **Human-in-Loop:** Ensuring professional oversight in AI decisions.

---

## 🏆 Challenges & Winning Formula
| Challenge | Winning Formula |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maternal Health Companion** | Clinical accuracy + safety + explainability. |
| **Health Worker Assistant** | Practical impact + offline usability. |
| **Telehealth Offline System** | Accessibility in low-connectivity zones. |
| **Risk Prediction Engine** | Early detection accuracy + explainable AI. |

---
