# 🌐 @-09-global-collaboration-framework

*The NRB + Bangladesh Builder Framework: Connecting Local Innovation with Global Expertise.*

---

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
**Embed Global Standards:** Ensuring that every team operates with international execution standards from Day One.

---

## 🤝 Participation Model
Every team is encouraged to include an **NRB (Non-Resident Bangladeshi) AI Expert** or an **International Technical Advisor**.

### 📍 NRB Contribution Areas
- **Architecture Design:** Scaling systems for global users.
- **Product Strategy:** Identifying product-market fit beyond local borders.
- **Compliance Insight:** Understanding global regulatory frameworks.
- **Deployment Standards:** Implementing enterprise-grade dev practices.

---

## ⚖️ Governance Rules
> [!IMPORTANT]
> - All NRB contributions must be **transparently documented**.
> - NRB advisors may guide but **must not manipulate** judging outcomes.

---

## 🏛️ Collaboration Format
- **Integrated Team Member:** Active participant in the build process.
- **Remote Advisor:** Periodic review and strategy guidance.
- **Architecture Reviewer:** Ensuring technical robustness.

---

## 🚀 Expected Outcome
> "Participants gain experience operating like globally distributed AI startup teams from day one."

### 📈 Competitive Advantage
- ✅ Alignment with global best practices.
- ✅ Market-ready positioning.
- ✅ Cross-border scaling capability.
