# 🗺️ @-03-innovation-tracks-overview

*Five strategic tracks designed to solve global problems through AI execution.*

---

## 🚦 The Track Matrix
Participants must select **ONE** domain for their challenge. While you can bring your own idea, only those aligned with the tracks will be considered for awards.

| Track | Name | Strategic Theme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **EdTech** | Adaptive, inclusive, and scalable learning ecosystems. |
| **02** | **MarTech** | Multimodal, AI-first brand intelligence platforms. |
| **03** | **HealthTech** | Accessible, ethical AI-enabled healthcare solutions. |
| **04** | **E-Commerce** | AI-native commerce intelligence for SMEs. |
| **05** | **InfoTech** | Digital trust, validation, and misinformation control. |

---

## 🛠️ The Global Collaboration Layer
Every track is designed with a **Global Collaboration Model** in mind.
- 🧬 **NRB Integration:** Teams are encouraged to include an NRB expert.
- 📜 **Global Standards:** Solutions should align with international regulations (e.g., WHO for HealthTech).

---

## ✨ Selection Rules
> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must select ONE track. Custom ideas outside these tracks may be showcased but are NOT eligible for awards.
