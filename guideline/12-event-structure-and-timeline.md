# 📅 @-12-event-structure-and-timeline

*A roadmap from idea formation to national AI system execution.*

---

## 🗓️ Phase 1: Open Registration
- **Focus:** Nationwide participation and global collaboration.
- **Activities:** Team formation (3–5 members), role assignment, NRB integration.
- **Outcome:** Diverse teams combining technical and product capabilities.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 2: Preliminary Submission
- **Focus:** Merit-based screening of the most promising solutions.
- **Activities:** 1-page project summary + 3-minute concept/prototype video.
- **Deadline:** ⏰ **May 15, 2026**
- **Outcome:** Selection of the **Top 200 teams**.

---

## 🗓️ Phase 3: Physical BuildFest
- **Focus:** The national stage of presentation and networking.
- **Activities:** Demos, judging, mentor sessions, and **Opportunity Connect**.
- **Date:** 📅 **June 12, 2026**
- **Outcome:** Recognition of winners and connection to career/startup pathways.

---

## ⚡ Infinity 5 Prompt Challenge
- **Focus:** Master of prompt-driven engineering.
- **Date:** June 12, 2026
- **Task:** Build a working AI application using **max 5 prompts** with Lovable or Cursor.

---

> [!TIP]
> This structured sequence ensures participants move from early ideas to deployable AI solutions with global support.
